# Project Name: CashFlow Copilot

" Predict. Understand. Simulate. Prevent. – 
Your AI-Powered Financial Decision Making Assistant that helps you Test the Decision before you Take the Decision."

Problem Statement:-

                       PROFITABILITY ≠ CASH AVAILABILITY

Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) often face unexpected cash shortages due to delayed customer payments, upcoming vendor dues, salary obligations, GST payments, and unplanned expenses. Traditional accounting tools mainly track past transactions rather than predict future financial risks, leaving business owners unprepared for potential liquidity crises. SMEs need an intelligent solution to anticipate financial challenges and make timely, informed decisions.



Proposed Solution:-

CashFlow Copilot is an AI-powered cash flow intelligence and automation platform that helps SMEs predict, prevent, and manage financial risks. It analyses receivables, payables, bank statements, fixed expenses, and historical payment patterns to generate 30/60/90-day cash flow forecasts, identify potential cash shortages, and estimate financial risk probabilities. Its intelligent recommendation engine suggests actionable solutions such as prioritising overdue payments, negotiating vendor extensions, and optimising expenses.

The platform also features an interactive ""What-If Simulator"", allowing business owners to simulate scenarios such as delayed customer payments, unexpected expenses, increased sales, or vendor payment extensions and instantly understand their potential impact on future cash flow. Additional capabilities include suspicious invoice detection and a 24/7 AI assistant supporting text, voice, and document-based interactions.



Unique Value Proposition:-

Unlike traditional accounting tools that only show where money went, CashFlow Copilot predicts what might happen next, allows businesses to explore possible financial scenarios, and recommends what to do before a crisis occurs.

Vision:-

Empower SMEs with accessible AI-driven financial intelligence for proactive decision-making, improved liquidity, and sustainable business growth.
